## 0. Quick Start Guide (Most Efficient Usage)

1. **First-time users**  
   Copy the prompt in **Section 2**, replace the title code(s), and send it to the AI. The AI will generate synopsis, tags, and score, and output rows that can be pasted directly into Excel.

2. **When you already have an Excel list**  
   Send the new code(s) together with this guide and ask the AI to “append rows using the existing Excel column structure” so the format stays consistent.

## 1. General Scoring Standards (Out of 10, two decimal places)

### Scoring Dimensions & Suggested Weights (adjustable)

| Dimension | Suggested Weight | Description |
|-----------|------------------|-------------|
| Production Scale & Finish | 18% | Cast size, scene variety, shooting quality, whether content is padded |
| Sexual Tension & Ero Finish | 18% | Overall erotic atmosphere, actress commitment, variety of play, sustained excitement |
| Actress Beauty | 15% | Looks, body proportions, presence, on-camera appeal |
| Actress Popularity | 12% | Presence of top-tier / leading actresses, fan base |
| Story Quality | 12% | Narrative completeness, pacing, emotional tension, immersion |
| Runtime & Content Density | 8% | Effective content ratio, concentration of highlight scenes |
| Acting & Emotional Expression | 7% | Facial expressions, breathing, naturalness and intensity of climax reactions |
| Freshness / Differentiation | 5% | Unique settings, uncommon play styles, avoidance of sameness |
| Series Positioning & Reputation | 5% | Whether it belongs to a classic series, sequel consistency, overall reputation |

**Notes**:  
The weights above are recommended defaults. Users may adjust them according to personal preference.  
Final score = sum of (each dimension score × corresponding weight), kept to two decimal places.

### Reference Score Ranges

- **9.20 – 9.60**: Industry top-tier productions, extremely high finish, strongly recommended  
- **8.50 – 9.15**: High-quality excellent works, worth prioritizing  
- **7.80 – 8.45**: Above-average level with clear strengths  
- **7.00 – 7.75**: Average level; watch selectively according to taste  
- **6.50 and below**: Average finish or heavily formulaic
